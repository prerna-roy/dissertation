# Statement on the Use of Generative AI

**Dissertation:** Prompt-engineering a Large Language Model for Data Extraction from New South Wales Coronial Findings and Recommendations

**Author:** Prerna Roy (z5650460), Master of Health Data Science, UNSW

**AI tool:** Claude (Anthropic), used through the Claude app

**Models:** Sonnet 5.5, Opus 5.5

This statement describes how a generative AI assistant was used during this project. 

## How the AI assistant was used

- **Background research:** literature searches and summaries on prompting techniques for information extraction (zero-shot, few-shot, and chain-of-thought prompting) and on evaluation metrics (exact match, ROUGE, BLEU, BERTScore, and LLM-as-a-judge methods such as FineSurE). These informed the design of the prompts and the scoring. The author read through all literature reviewed to ensure suitability for the study; the AI tool was largely used in a search capacity. 
- **Planning and tracking:** to-do lists, summaries of supervisor meetings, and status notes for each experimental stage. The author reviewed all outputs here to ensure progress was made for the study. 
- **Code:** help building and debugging the prompting and evaluation pipelines in Python. In the final stage, the notebooks' markdown and comments were tidied with the tool, file paths were aligned with this repository, and the README, requirements file, and `.gitignore` were drafted. The code itself was not changed in after the final pass. The author reviewed all code themselves before using it. 
- **Checking results:** checking the numbers reported in the dissertation against the evaluation workbooks and audit logs. This included tracing the version history of the reference standard, checking figures and tables, and tidying the supplementary workbook (`results/final_report_tables_clean.xlsx`) used to calculate the reported results. The results were obtained and analysed by the author themselves. The supplementary workbook produced tidies up the key results and findings from this study.
- **Reviewing the writing:** reviewing drafts of the Methods, Results, Discussion, and Appendices. The assistant suggested wording, flagged claims that were inconsistent or wrongly worded with the results, checked citations against their source papers, and checked cross-references to sections, tables, figures, appendices, and the reference list. The author wrote the paper themselves.
- **Figure geneartion:** some figures used in the final dissertation were generated by the AI assistant. The author has put in the captions of these generated figures that they were generated by Claude (Opus 5.5). 

The author designed the study and research questions, guided by the supervisors of the project. The selection and annotation of reports, creation of the reference standard, development of prompts, review of model disagreements in the human review stage, interpretation of all results, and final decisions on content and wording. 

All suggestions made by the AI assistant were always carefully reviewed by the author and cross-checked with their own work.
